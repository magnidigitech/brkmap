## One architectural change I'd make now

Before building V3, I'd refactor the optimizer into a clearly isolated service:

```text
src/lib/optimizer/

├── optimizer.ts
├── constraints.ts
├── validation.ts
├── scoring.ts
├── clustering.ts
├── alternatives.ts
├── risk.ts
├── buffer.ts
└── types.ts
```

And routing:

```text
src/lib/routing/

├── provider.ts
├── google/
│   ├── routes.ts
│   ├── matrix.ts
│   ├── places.ts
│   └── geocoding.ts
├── cache.ts
└── types.ts
```

That will make the codebase much easier to maintain as live tracking and AI features are added.

### The key transition for MapOptima V3 is:

**V1:** Plan the route
**V2:** Find the best feasible schedule
**V3:** **Monitor the real campaign day and automatically react when reality differs from the plan**
**V4:** Predict and assist

That progression gives you a very strong product direction instead of turning the application into a collection of unrelated Google Maps features.
